Consistency Checker

Scoring and streaks

Tracking

  • Tracking starts on October 18, 2026.
  • Earlier dates are outside scoring: no misses, breaks or "?".

Daily Consistency

  • Gym + Reading = 1
  • Gym only = 0.5
  • Reading only = 0.5
  • No activity = 0, and it breaks the live streak immediately.
  • A red day with Reading is a valid Reading-only 0.5 day.

Streak

  • Up to 3 consecutive half-days are allowed.
  • The 4th consecutive half-day breaks the streak and is not counted.
  • A full 1-point day resets the half-day count.
  • After a break, the next qualifying activity starts a new streak.
  • A streak carries across gold result days, from month to month, and from December into January.

Monthly result

  • The final calendar day of every month is the gold result day. Tap it to see that month's result.
  • No Gym or Reading can be added on the gold day.
  • The gold day is not scored and does not change the streak or the half-day count.
  • Only the streak still alive at the month's last scored day counts toward that month's Consistency result.
  • If the streak breaks during the month, the part before the break does not count in that month's result.

Calendar

  • Green = Gym data exists. Green is automatic.
  • Red = no Gym. On a day with no Gym, a tap switches unmarked ↔ red.
  • A Gym save on a red day is refused until the red mark is removed.
  • Black stripe = Reading data exists.
  • ? = the day's calendar mark conflicts with its Gym data. That date is left out of everything until corrected.

Gym growth

  • Each Gym row = sets × reps.
  • A day's Gym value is its single highest row. Rows are never added together.
  • Gym Contribution = the sum of those daily values on Gym days in the surviving streak.
  • Average Gym Performance = Gym Contribution ÷ Gym days.

Reading growth

  • A day's Reading value is the number of different books read that day. The same book entered several times counts once.
  • Pages are saved but are not used for scoring.
  • Reading Contribution = the sum of those daily values on Reading days in the surviving streak.
  • Average Reading Breadth = Reading Contribution ÷ Reading days.

History and corrections

  • Month-to-month comparisons and history stay within one calendar year. January does not compare with December.
  • The carried streak still continues from December into January.
  • Correcting past data recalculates every result it affects.
  • A completed month that still has "?" dates shows Unresolved month instead of a final number.
